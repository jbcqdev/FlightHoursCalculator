Roster App – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 October 2026

Roster keeps all your data on your iPhone. It has no accounts, no servers, no analytics and no advertising, and it does not send your roster or notes anywhere.

This policy applies to the Roster iOS app ("the app"), which imports crew roster PDFs and shows your duties by day and in your iPhone calendar.

Information the app handles

The app only handles information you choose to give it. All of it is stored inside the app on your device.

Information Where it comes from What it is used for
Roster PDFs You import them from Files or the share sheet (e.g. Mail) Reading your duties, flights, layovers and training
Details in the roster Read from the PDF: name, staff ID, rank, fleet, roster period, flights, sectors, times, duty codes, training and remarks Showing your roster by day and creating calendar events
PDF issue date The PDF's own creation or modification date Deciding which roster applies when imported rosters overlap
Your notes Typed by you on a day's detail screen Shown in the app and added to the matching calendar event
Settings Your choices in the app, such as which calendar to sync to Remembering your preferences

The app gets time zones from a table built into the app. It does not use your location.

Calendar access

The app asks for permission to access your calendars so it can add your duties to the one calendar you pick. You can refuse or withdraw this permission at any time in iOS Settings. The app still works without it, but calendar sync is unavailable.

When you sync, the app:

  • Adds events for flights (including positioning), simulator, ground duties and meetings, plus all-day events for leave and days off.
  • Puts your notes, time zone details and roster information in each event's description.
  • Marks every event it creates with "Added by Roster app" in its notes.
  • Deletes only events carrying that mark, and only on dates covered by your imported rosters, before adding the updated ones.

To find its own events, the app checks events on those dates on your device. It never changes, deletes or stores any other event. It does not create events for layovers or hotels.

What the app does not collect

The app has no servers and makes no network requests of its own. It does not:

  • Collect analytics, usage statistics or crash reports.
  • Show advertising or track you across apps or websites.
  • Access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • Require an account or sign-in, including Google sign-in.
  • Include third-party code, SDKs or libraries.

The developer cannot see your roster, notes or any other data in the app.

Storage, retention and deletion

Your imported rosters, notes and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your iPhone, protected by iOS. They stay there until you remove them.

  • Remove one roster: in Settings, swipe to delete it from the list of imported rosters.
  • Remove everything: delete the app. This erases all rosters, notes and settings stored by the app.
  • Calendar events: events already added to your calendar stay there after you delete the app. You can delete them in the Calendar app. They are marked "Added by Roster app".

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, iOS may include the app's data in that backup. Those backups are handled by Apple or your computer under your own settings, not by the app.

Sharing with third parties

The app does not sell, share or transfer your information to anyone.

The one indirect path is your calendar. If the calendar you sync to belongs to an account set up in iOS (such as iCloud, Google or Exchange), iOS syncs the events the app adds to that account's provider. That syncing is done by iOS under the provider's own privacy policy, not by the app. To keep your roster on the device only, choose a calendar stored "On My iPhone".

Children

The app is made for airline crew and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of data changes, this policy will be updated and the effective date above revised. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
